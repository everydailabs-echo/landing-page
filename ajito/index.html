How Ajito works

Three steps, no setup grind. Ajito gets smarter every time you use it.

1

Plan a week in one chat

Open the planner, tell Ajito your week. It proposes seven dinners anchored to what's already in your pantry — pin the ones you like, swap the ones you don't.

2

A shopping list, automatically

Ajito reconciles every recipe against your pantry. Uncheck what you've already got — the rest lands on your shopping list, with quantities scaled to your portions.

3

Shop with confidence

Open shop mode at the store. Tap each item as you grab it — it strikes through and moves straight to your pantry, ready for tonight.

Why I built Ajito

Most nights, the hardest part of cooking wasn't the cooking — it was deciding what to cook.

I tried the general AI assistants. They gave me decent recipes, but every conversation started from scratch. I had to re-explain my pantry, my family's tastes, what I cooked last Tuesday, every single time.

I wanted an AI that remembered. One that knew what was in my fridge, what my kids would actually eat, and what we'd had this week. So I built one.

— Chase, founder

Frequently asked

What happens after the 14-day trial?

You choose a tier and start paying. We never auto-charge a card — the trial requires no card on signup, so you'll only be billed if you actively subscribe.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings or by emailing us. You keep access through the end of the period you've already paid for.

What languages does Ajito speak?

English, Japanese, and French — all fully supported including pantry items, recipes, plan dates, and the shopping list interface. You can switch languages anytime.

What about taxes?

All prices shown are tax-inclusive (Japan Consumption Tax 10% built in). Customers outside Japan see prices in their local currency at checkout, with applicable local tax handled by our payment provider Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record.

Where does my data live?

Account data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the Tokyo (ap-northeast-1) region. AI requests are processed by Anthropic under zero-retention terms — your chats aren't used to train models. Full privacy policy.

How long does the founder rate last?

The 25% discount applies for the first 12 months of your subscription. After 12 months, your subscription continues at the standard rate.
