Frequently asked

What happens after the 14-day trial?

At the end of the trial, you choose a tier and start paying. We never auto-charge a card — the trial requires no card on signup, so you'll only be billed if you actively subscribe.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings or by emailing us. You keep access through the end of the period you've already paid for.

What about taxes?

All prices shown above are tax-inclusive (Japan Consumption Tax 10% built in). Customers outside Japan see prices in their local currency at checkout, with applicable local tax (US sales tax, EU VAT, etc.) handled by our payment provider Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record.

Will my card issuer add any extra fees?

The price on this page is the full amount captured by Lemon Squeezy — we don't add any surcharge on top. However, Lemon Squeezy is registered as a U.S.-based Merchant of Record, so some Japanese-issued credit cards apply a small international-transaction fee (typically ~1–2%, varies by issuer) on any charge from a foreign merchant, even when the charge currency is JPY. This fee is set and collected entirely by your card issuer; it does not go to us or to Lemon Squeezy, and the exact amount depends on your card. To avoid it, check whether your card offers a "no-foreign-transaction-fee" option, or contact us about alternative payment methods.

How long does the founder rate last?

The 25% discount applies for the first 12 months of your subscription. After 12 months, your subscription continues at the standard rate (Solo ¥980/mo, Family ¥1,980/mo). The standard rate may change in the future; in that case we'll notify all subscribers at least 30 days before any change takes effect. If you cancel and later return, the founder discount no longer applies.

What if I exceed my monthly token limit?

Token usage is tracked per billing period. If you reach the limit, you can either upgrade to a higher tier mid-period or wait until your next renewal. We'll always warn you well before you hit the cap.

Do you store my photos or recipes long-term?

Photos are processed by our AI provider and not retained on our servers after the response. Recipes you choose to save stay in your private library and can be exported or deleted at any time. See our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 May 2026

What we collect. EverydAI Labs operates products under separate brands. The website itself uses no marketing or behavior-tracking cookies, no third-party analytics, and no advertising pixels. Minimal technical cookies and data processing required for security and payment (via Cloudflare for edge protection and Lemon Squeezy for checkout sessions) do occur and are out of our direct control; their use is limited to the function each provider performs. Product-specific data handling is described below.

Ajito (cooking assistant). When you create an Ajito account we collect:

  • Your email address — used to send one-time login codes (we do not store passwords) and account notifications
  • A locale preference
  • Profile data that you enter into the app:
    • Pantry & inventory. Pantry items and your expiry-tracking preference.
    • Personalization. Cooking goals, recipe browse / save history, and an exclusion list of ingredients you'd like Ajito to leave out of suggestions. We process exclusion entries as free-text strings to filter on — we do not interpret, categorize, or store any inference about whether an entry reflects a medical, religious, or other reason. Ajito is a culinary preference tool, not a medical safety system; please verify ingredient labels yourself for any genuine health-related restriction.
    • Household. Household composition (number of adults and children only — we do not collect names, birthdates, or other individually identifying information about household members).
  • Photos of fridges, receipts, or dishes that you submit. We handle this image data as follows:
    • External transfer. Image data is sent to an external AI provider (currently Anthropic, Inc.) for analysis, for the purposes of service provision and functional improvement. We operate under zero-retention terms with this provider — neither the input data nor the response is retained on their side after the analysis completes.
    • Our servers. Submitted images are stored on our Supabase infrastructure in the Tokyo region for up to 30 days, after which an automated cleanup job permanently deletes them.
    • Extracted data. Text and pantry-delta information extracted from the image is stored persistently on your account, and is removed alongside all other account data when you delete your account.
  • Standard server logs (request timestamps, IP, error traces) retained up to 30 days for abuse prevention and debugging

Where data is stored. Ajito account data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the Tokyo (ap-northeast-1) region. AI requests are processed by Anthropic (Claude API) under their zero-retention terms — Anthropic does not retain the content of your requests beyond the response.

Data security. All personal data we process is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored encrypted at rest by our infrastructure providers.

Sub-processors. We use the following service providers to deliver Ajito. Each operates under its own privacy policy; in handling your data on our behalf, they act as sub-processors under our terms.

  • Supabase — primary database, file storage, authentication. Tokyo region.
  • Anthropic PBC — AI model inference (Claude). US-hosted, under zero-retention terms.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (one-time codes, account notifications). US-hosted with EU region available.
  • Fly.io — application hosting. Tokyo region.
  • Cloudflare — DNS, edge network, basic DDoS protection. Global.
  • Lemon Squeezy — payment processing and merchant of record. US-hosted.

If this list changes, we will update this page before the new sub-processor begins handling your data.

Cross-border data transfer. Some of the sub-processors above are located outside Japan (Anthropic, Resend, Cloudflare, and Lemon Squeezy in the United States). For each, we have reviewed the personal-data-protection framework of the receiving country and applied appropriate technical and contractual safeguards (including zero-retention terms with Anthropic, encrypted transport, and standard data-processing terms with each provider). Information about the data-protection regimes of these countries is available upon request to contact@everydailabs.com.

Payments. Subscription billing is processed by Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record. Lemon Squeezy collects payment instrument data, billing address, and tax information directly; we never receive or store full card numbers. Their privacy policy is available at lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

Your rights (GDPR / Japan APPI). You may at any time:

  • Export your account data by emailing contact@everydailabs.com
  • Delete your account and all associated data from within the Ajito app, or by emailing the same address (we respond within 30 days)
  • Object to processing or request correction of inaccurate data

Contact for privacy matters: contact@everydailabs.com
