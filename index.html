Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect. EverydAI Labs operates products under separate brands. The website itself uses no tracking cookies, no third-party analytics, and no advertising pixels. Product-specific data handling is described below.

Ajito (cooking assistant). When you create an Ajito account we collect:

  • Your email address (for login and service notifications)
  • A locale preference and your chosen display name
  • Pantry contents, dietary preferences, and recipe history that you enter or upload
  • Photos of fridges, receipts, or dishes that you submit. Images are passed to our AI provider for analysis and are not retained on our servers after the response is processed; only the extracted text or pantry deltas are persisted.
  • Standard server logs (request timestamps, IP, error traces) retained up to 30 days for abuse prevention and debugging

Where data is stored. Ajito account data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the Tokyo (ap-northeast-1) region. AI requests are processed by Anthropic (Claude API) under their zero-retention terms — Anthropic does not retain the content of your requests beyond the response.

Payments. Subscription billing is processed by Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record. Lemon Squeezy collects payment instrument data, billing address, and tax information directly; we never receive or store full card numbers. Their privacy policy is available at lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.

Your rights (GDPR / Japan APPI). You may at any time:

  • Export your account data by emailing contact@everydailabs.com
  • Delete your account and all associated data from within the Ajito app, or by emailing the same address (we respond within 30 days)
  • Object to processing or request correction of inaccurate data

Contact for privacy matters: contact@everydailabs.com
